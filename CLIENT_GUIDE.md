# How to manage your new website

Hi [Client name],

Your website is ready. Here is a short guide to the three things you will most likely need.

## 1. Changing text or prices

The easiest way: email me what you want changed (for example, "change the second service description to…" or "add the price $150 to Garden Design"). I will update the site, usually within [1 business day].

If you prefer to do it yourself:

1. Find the file called **index.html** in your website folder.
2. Right-click it, choose **Open with → Notepad**.
3. Find the sentence you want to change and edit only the words. Do not delete anything in angle brackets, like `<p>` or `</p>`.
4. Save the file (Ctrl+S) and double-click it to preview. If something looks broken, press Ctrl+Z, or just send me the file and I will fix it.

The new text appears on the live site after I upload it, so please send me the saved file.

## 2. Checking that the contact form works

1. Open your website and scroll to "Request a free quote."
2. Fill in the form with your own name, phone number and email, and write a short test message, such as "Test."
3. Click **Send request**. You should see: *"Thank you, we'll contact you within 24 hours."*
4. Within a few minutes, check the inbox of [form email address]. Also look in your Spam folder. You should see your test message.

If the green thank-you message does not appear, or the email does not arrive within 15 minutes, please contact me. Do a quick test like this once a month.
